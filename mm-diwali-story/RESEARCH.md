# Research brief (inspiration + craft rules the build must follow)

## What gets forwarded (Indian Diwali, 2019-2025)
- Make the viewer the subject: Cadbury "Not Just a Cadbury Ad" (Ogilvy) let SRK name *your* local shop -> forwarded because it was about the sender/receiver. MM version: "this hoarding is booked for YOU tonight".
- The billboard itself as the joke: Flipkart Minutes "Diwali ka Ladoo, Halloween ka Kadoo" (DDB Mudra 2024) billboard flips day/night; Amul topicals (<= 8 words, the hoarding itself becomes the scene, e.g. dahi-handi pyramid climbing the board); Zomato x Blinkit billboard banter.
- Soan papdi regifting is India's most reliable Diwali in-joke (Swiggy 2019, Flipkart GPS-tracked soan papdi billboards, Zepto 2024 "Make Soan Papdi Great Again", 2025 "Mithai Wars").
- Fresh = specific small truths (Diwali safai & mummy's "thoda aur", tangled lights, office rangoli, "shaadi kab?", bonus in kaju katli, dog vs crackers). Cringe = diya + swirly font + "May light triumph over darkness", template gold sparkle, cracker lectures.
- Billboard-length lines (<= 6 words): "Lights untangled. Marriage still intact." / "Rangoli: 3 hours. Dog: 3 seconds." / "Ghar chamak gaya. Mummy: 'Thoda aur.'" / "Bonus aaya. Kaju katli roop mein." / "Diet starts after Bhai Dooj." / "Soan papdi: India's most forwarded content." / bridge line: "Every balcony is a billboard tonight."

## Craft that does NOT look AI-made
- Deliberate imperfection: block-print/letterpress misregistration (1-2 px plate offsets), paper grain, dust, uneven ink, hand-made wobble. Animate textures/wobble "on twos" (12 fps) while the camera stays smooth at 30 fps.
- Palette discipline: night indigo/ink + marigold/gold light + paper off-white, MM Red as the ONLY red. Bloom only on true light sources (high threshold, already enforced by the finishing pass).
- Type lands on the beat, >= 1.5-2 s per line, ~3 words/s max reading speed.
- One continuous, motivated camera (2.5D parallax, push-ins); no random spins or generic zooms.
- WhatsApp recompresses: coarse ~2 px grain, slightly lifted blacks (done in post), avoid hairline details < 2 px, avoid big flat dark gradients without texture.
- References: Studio Eeksaurus (handmade clay/sand/wool commercial animation), HandpaintedType / Indian sign-painter lettering for hoarding copy feel.

## Music & sound
- Shehnai = "mangal vadya" (auspicious): a short shehnai phrase at 0 s signals festivity instantly.
- Groove: keherwa (8-beat, 4+4) for the festive body; dhol/dholak + tabla, manjira/ghungroo shimmer, claps, one temple bell, santoor sparkle. Crackers as percussion (whoosh/crackle ON the beat).
- 120 BPM -> 1 bar = 2.0 s, beat = 0.5 s: snap every visual beat to this grid.
- Scale: Raag Yaman (C D E F# G A B; evening, lamp-lighting) or Bhupali pentatonic (C D E G A) for hooks; Khamaj (b7) for a playful middle. No film-song melodies.
- End on a tihai (phrase x3) landing exactly on the logo/end-card downbeat.

## Story format
- 20-26 s = one segment on Instagram Stories (60 s cards) and WhatsApp Status (90 s since 2025).
- Safe zone: keep text/logo out of top ~250 px and bottom ~320 px; ~6% side margins.
- Hook: frame 0 already shows something readable/funny + a sound hit. No fade from black, no logo-first open; MM can appear early but quietly (agency plate on the hoarding, like real hoardings).
- End: hold 2-3 s end card (greeting + lockup + "Trust, made visible.") - the screenshot frame; a natural forward nudge; last frame should echo the first for loops.
